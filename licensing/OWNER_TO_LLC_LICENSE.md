# Victory Owner-to-Company Authorization

**Effective date:** September 20, 2026

This document records and confirms the permission that **Grant A. Murray** (“Owner”) gives to **Discovery Games Interactive LLC** (“Company”) concerning Victory Theater VTT and related first-party materials. Owner is also the person authorized to act for Company. This authorization is effective without separate signatures and remains part of the project’s licensing records.

## 1. Ownership

Owner retains ownership of Victory Theater VTT, the Victory names and marks, Socio, Niava, Catharsis Theater, Victory documentation, and other first-party materials that Owner creates or owns (“Licensed Property”).

Nothing in this agreement assigns ownership to Company. Goodwill arising from use of the Victory names and marks benefits Owner.

## 2. Royalty-free permission to Company

Owner grants Company a nonexclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to:

- use, reproduce, modify, display, perform, distribute, host, market, demonstrate, and commercially exploit the Licensed Property;
- operate Victory and related products and services for Company’s own revenue-producing activities;
- publish and administer the Victory Community License, Victory Commercial Terms, and related policies on Owner’s behalf;
- issue nonexclusive sublicenses to customers and other users under published or custom terms;
- collect and retain licensing, subscription, hosting, service, support, development, and other commercial fees; and
- use and administer the Victory names and marks consistently with Owner’s policies.

Company may not assign ownership of the Licensed Property, register it as Company’s property, or grant an exclusive or perpetual sublicense unless Owner separately agrees in writing.

## 3. Term and revocation

This license begins on the effective date above and continues until Owner revokes it by giving Company at least ninety days’ written notice.

During the notice period, Company may continue ordinary operations and issue licenses in the normal course of business, but it may not deliberately create unusually long or expansive commitments intended to defeat the revocation.

## 4. Existing customer licenses survive

A customer or user license validly issued before Company’s authority ends remains valid according to its existing term and conditions. Company may perform obligations already owed under that license.

After Company’s authority ends, it may not issue new licenses, renew expired licenses, materially expand existing licenses, or represent that it remains Owner’s authorized commercial administrator unless Owner gives new written permission.

## 5. Records and cooperation

Company will maintain reasonable records of sublicenses it issues and, after notice of revocation, provide Owner the information reasonably necessary to identify and honor surviving licenses.

## 6. No required royalty

Company owes Owner no royalty or license fee under this authorization. Owner may change that arrangement only through a later written record made on behalf of both Owner and Company; no change will retroactively affect sublicenses already issued.

## 7. No warranties

Owner provides the Licensed Property to Company as is and without warranties to the maximum extent permitted by law.

## 8. General terms

This authorization is the complete record between Owner and Company concerning this royalty-free license. Changes must be recorded in writing by Owner acting personally and, when Company rights or obligations are affected, on Company’s behalf.

California law governs, excluding conflict-of-laws rules. Exclusive jurisdiction and venue lie in the state and federal courts located in Orange County, California.

## Record of authorization

Grant A. Murray, as Owner and as the person authorized to act for Discovery Games Interactive LLC, directed that this authorization be incorporated into the Victory Theater VTT licensing package. No separate signature block is required by this record.
