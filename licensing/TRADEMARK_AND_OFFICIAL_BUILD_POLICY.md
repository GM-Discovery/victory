# Victory Trademark and Official Build Policy 1.0

**Trademark owner:** Grant A. Murray  
**Authorized commercial administrator:** Discovery Games Interactive LLC

This policy covers the names and marks **Victory**, **Victory Theater**, **Victory Theater VTT**, associated logos, and the distinctive visual identity used to identify official Victory software and services (“Victory Marks”).

## 1. Truthful references

You may use Victory Marks truthfully to:

- identify an unmodified official release;
- state that an independent work is compatible with Victory;
- link to the canonical Victory repository or website;
- describe, review, criticize, or discuss Victory; and
- provide attribution required by the Victory Community License.

Examples include “compatible with Victory Theater VTT,” “a module for Victory,” and “derived from Victory Theater VTT.”

## 2. Victory-branded builds

A distributed build may use Victory as its product name only when it:

1. is an unmodified official release; or
2. clearly identifies itself as an unofficial modified Victory build, complies with the applicable software license, and retains every package required by the Official Build Manifest.

Adding other game systems, settings, venues, or modules does not violate this policy.

## 3. Required official packages

Every Victory-branded build must retain complete, functional, normally accessible copies of:

- the official Socio rules package;
- the official Catharsis Theater package;
- required license, attribution, privacy, and safety information; and
- any tutorial or onboarding material designated as essential in the Official Build Manifest.

An operator does not have to use, advertise, perform, teach, or present Socio or Catharsis Theater to its customers. Customers do not need to see or play either package. Inclusion preserves the identity and complete public inheritance of Victory.

## 4. Noncommercial renamed forks

A lawful noncommercial fork may remove or materially impair a required official package, but it must:

- choose a product name that does not include or imitate a Victory Mark;
- remove Victory logos and trade dress that would suggest an official release;
- use Victory Marks only for truthful attribution, such as “derived from Victory Theater VTT”;
- preserve all copyright and license notices; and
- state prominently that Grant A. Murray and Discovery Games Interactive LLC do not sponsor or endorse the fork.

## 5. Commercial renamed and stripped forks

Commercial Use of a renamed, unbranded, white-label, or materially package-stripped fork requires a custom written license from Grant A. Murray or his authorized commercial administrator, currently Discovery Games Interactive LLC.

Removing Victory Marks satisfies a trademark condition; it does not expand copyright or commercial-use permission.

## 6. Written permission required

Written permission is required to:

- name a business, hosted service, domain, application, or product using a Victory Mark;
- suggest sponsorship, certification, partnership, or endorsement;
- register a confusingly similar trademark, domain name, social-media identifier, or software-package name;
- use official logos on merchandise or paid promotional goods; or
- operate a commercial white-label or renamed fork.

## 7. Lawful speech

This policy does not prohibit fair use, commentary, criticism, parody, comparative advertising, or other uses permitted by law.
