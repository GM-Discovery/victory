Discovery Games Interactive LLC does not individually negotiate or approve an ordinary use that qualifies for a published tier and complies with these terms.

## 2. License Year

For free Small Commercial Use, a “License Year” is the twelve-month period beginning when you first receive Victory-Related Revenue, followed by consecutive twelve-month periods.

For a paid standard commercial license, the License Year is the twelve-month term beginning on the license’s effective date.

## 3. Victory-Related Revenue

“Victory-Related Revenue” means gross revenue received from any production, performance, service, event, hosting arrangement, or other commercial activity in which Victory materially supports creation, preparation, administration, presentation, participation, or delivery.

Revenue counts regardless of which Victory venue, game system, rules package, or feature is visible to the customer. Ticket revenue from a production presented through First Theater counts even when its customers never see or play Socio or Catharsis Theater.

Victory-Related Revenue includes:

- admission, player, participation, production, facilitation, and professional game-master fees;
- subscriptions and paid memberships materially connected to the activity;
- sponsorship and advertising revenue materially connected to the activity;
- crowdfunding, patronage, or donations that purchase access, participation, prominence, benefits, or deliverables;
- hosted-service and technical-support fees involving Victory; and
- the portion of a bundled payment reasonably attributable to an activity materially supported by Victory.

The following do not count:

- separately stated sales, use, value-added, or similar taxes collected for a government;
- refunds actually paid;
- direct, reasonable expenses reimbursed without markup; and
- unsolicited donations that provide no access, participation, prominence, benefit, or deliverable in return.

Platform fees, payment-processing fees, wages, contractor payments, equipment costs, travel, art costs, and other business expenses do not reduce gross revenue.

## 4. Aggregation

Revenue received by people or entities under common ownership or control for the same production, service, brand, or substantially related activity must be counted together. Dividing revenue among collaborators, affiliated entities, accounts, venues, or installations does not create separate thresholds.

Unrelated clients of an independently operating professional do not become affiliates merely because they hire the same person.

## 5. Standard commercial permission

After payment of the applicable published tier, Discovery Games Interactive LLC, acting under authority granted by Grant A. Murray, grants the licensee a nonexclusive, nontransferable, non-sublicensable right during the License Year to operate official Victory-branded installations for the licensee’s own qualifying Commercial Use.

Players, audience members, cast, and crew may interact with a properly licensed production without buying separate licenses unless they independently operate or commercially exploit Victory.

## 6. Required official packages

Every installation operated under a standard commercial license must retain complete, functional, normally accessible copies of the official Socio and Catharsis Theater packages listed in that release’s Official Build Manifest.

The licensee does not have to use, advertise, perform, teach, display to customers, or earn revenue through either package. The packages must simply remain intact and available as part of the licensed Victory product.

## 7. Crossing a tier

If Victory-Related Revenue unexpectedly exceeds the licensed tier, the licensee has thirty days to upgrade and pay only the difference between the amount already paid for that License Year and the newly applicable tier.

Crossing a tier does not retroactively breach the license when the upgrade occurs within that period.

If revenue exceeds $250,000, the licensee must contact Discovery Games Interactive LLC within thirty days. Existing use may continue for sixty days after contact while the parties consider custom terms, provided the licensee acts in good faith and continues complying with all other terms.

## 8. Custom license required

Published tiers do not authorize:

- Commercial Use of a renamed or unbranded Victory fork;
- Commercial Use of a build that removes or materially impairs an official Socio or Catharsis Theater package;
- a white-label version;
- reselling, sublicensing, renting, or commercially distributing Victory itself;
- hosting Victory installations for unrelated paying operators;
- incorporating Victory into another commercial software product or managed platform;
- transferring the license to another operator; or
- Victory-Related Revenue exceeding $250,000 during the License Year.

Each requires a custom written agreement from Grant A. Murray or his authorized commercial administrator. Removing Victory names or marks does not grant or expand commercial permission.

If the licensee publicly distributes modifications, the Victory Community License’s notice, corresponding-source, same-license, and branding requirements apply unless a custom written agreement states otherwise.

## 11. Renewal and price changes

A paid license remains valid at its purchased price through its stated License Year. Future price changes do not retroactively alter that term. Renewal uses the price published when the renewal begins unless Discovery Games Interactive LLC has made a separate written commitment.

## 12. Inflation adjustments

“CPI-U” means the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, U.S. City Average, All Items, not seasonally adjusted, published by the United States Bureau of Labor Statistics.

The “Reference Index” is the CPI-U published for the calendar month immediately preceding the date on which these Commercial Terms first become effective. The “Current Index” is the CPI-U published for the same calendar month in the year before an adjustment takes effect.

Beginning on the first anniversary of these Commercial Terms and no more than once per year, the owner or authorized commercial administrator may publish inflation-adjusted thresholds and prices calculated as follows:

> Adjusted Amount = Base Amount × Current Index ÷ Reference Index

Revenue thresholds will be rounded to the nearest $100. License prices will be rounded to the nearest whole dollar. No inflation adjustment will reduce an amount below its Base Amount.

The owner or authorized commercial administrator must publish each adjusted schedule at least thirty days before it takes effect and include the index values and calculation used. An adjustment applies only when a new or renewed License Year begins. It does not alter the price, threshold, or scope of a License Year already in force.

If the United States Bureau of Labor Statistics materially changes or discontinues CPI-U, the owner or authorized commercial administrator may use the official successor index or the most closely comparable broad United States consumer-price index and must identify the replacement publicly.
