## 3. Discrepancies / cannot confirm

Recorded here rather than silently resolved, per spec §1.3 ("if an expected kernel cannot be proven, do not invent it; mark the historical gap explicitly"):

1. **`operator-log.md` has no chronological entries for Kernels 75, 76, 77, or 77A** — it jumps from the Kernel 74 header directly to Kernel 78. The reportbacks for that span are real, internally consistent, and self-cite migrations that exist — treat the reportbacks, not `operator-log.md`, as authoritative for 75–77A (matching `current-state.md`'s own stated convention for that exact span).
2. **Migration `088` does not exist** — the sequence runs `087_kernel79a_ewrite_skill_directory.sql` → `089_kernel79_goalc_object_link_types.sql`. No reportback among the 12 read explains the gap. Confirmed harmless (the migration runner sorts by filename and tracks a checksum ledger, not contiguous numbering — see `internal/migrate/migrate.go`), but the gap itself is unexplained and not invented an explanation for here.
3. **Migration `093_fix_missing_permission_requests_table.sql` is not cited by name in any Kernel 76–83 reportback.** It sits between Kernel 81 (092) and Kernel 81A (094). Likely an out-of-band hotfix — do not attribute it to a specific kernel without further evidence.
4. **The Canonical Roadmap's pre-84 Kernel 77/78 sections are planning-era, not implementation-era**, and diverge from what actually shipped: the roadmap's committed "Kernel 77" title ("Hosted-User Stabilization and Critical Security Repair") survives only as the superseded draft spec, folded into the real "Private-Client Readiness" scope; the roadmap's "Kernel 78" title ("Victory Documents Foundation") shipped as "eWrite Foundation" — the product was renamed at build time. Both are corrected in the roadmap update this kernel makes.
5. **Kernel 81A has no filed spec and no standalone reportback** — its only evidence is a ~10-line `operator-log.md` entry, migration 094, and its own test file. Real (live-deployed, tested) but documented more thinly than every other kernel in this range. Cite the operator-log entry directly; do not retroactively manufacture a spec or reportback for it.
