# Crew

**What it is:** a bounded, backstage support role. Crew can *see* everything backstage — the same visibility Director/Producer/Operator get — but cannot manage the Show. Crew's authority to actually *change* anything is limited to a short, specific list of non-destructive tasks.

**Home:** wherever the backstage work is — merchant/equipment authoring, cue triggering, and Scene composition surfaces, depending on what a given production has asked Crew to help with.

**Common actions:** author merchant dialogue/equipment content, trigger cues, help arrange Scene composition — always within tasks a Director/Producer has explicitly set up for Crew to touch.

**What it does not control:** roster, Show-level settings, Showing creation, or anything outside its specific assigned tasks. Crew visibility is broad; Crew authority is narrow and task-scoped by design — "always scoped, never a general management role."

**Contextual help:** Guide content appears at the specific backstage surface Crew is working in.
