# Producer

**What it is:** production-management authority at the Location level — scheduling, roster, and Showings, the business/administrative side of running a Show. Shares the same underlying authority as Director; many people are both.

**Home:** Producer's Office, Storyboards/Library for planning, Show Runs administration.

**Common actions:** create and schedule Shows/Showings, manage roster, configure audience admissions, oversee Scene Library and Storyboards for a production.

**What it does not control:** installation/infrastructure settings — that authority belongs only to the Operator, and is a genuinely separate thing from being a Producer. A Producer does not need any infrastructure knowledge to do their job.

**Contextual help:** Guide content appears in Producer's Office and at each administrative surface (roster, Showings, scheduling).
