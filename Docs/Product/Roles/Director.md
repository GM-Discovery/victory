# Director

**What it is:** live production-management authority for a Show. Directors run the moment-to-moment performance — Scene changes, cues, stage-object control — while a Showing is happening.

**Home:** Director's Chair (prep) and the live venue itself (Catharsis / First Theater / Victory Theater) during a Showing.

**Common actions:** configure and switch Scenes, trigger cues, reveal/hide stage objects, manage live stage state, see full backstage information.

**What it does not control:** infrastructure/installation settings (that's Operator) or things outside the Show(s) they manage at their Location. Director and Producer share the same underlying management authority — Victory doesn't separately gate "Director-only" actions from "Producer-only" actions; the distinction is about who's doing what in practice, not a hard technical wall.

**Contextual help:** Guide content appears in Director's Chair and inline during live-session tools.
