# Audience

**What it is:** the default, spectator role. You're Audience for any Showing you're admitted to but not rostered on as Cast or Crew.

**Home:** Catharsis, First Theater, and Victory Theater — wherever the Showing you're admitted to is being performed.

**Common actions:** watch the live performance, see whatever is projected to your assigned cohort (if the Show uses cohorts), participate in Audience-facing chat/reactions where a Show enables them.

**What it does not control:** Scene composition, stage objects, cues, roster, or anything backstage. Admission is per-Showing — being admitted to one Showing doesn't carry over to another.

**Contextual help:** Guide content appears in-venue when you first join a live Showing.
