# Cast (Player)

**What it is:** a rostered, active participant in a specific Show — you play a Character in it, rather than watching it. Your Cast standing comes from being on that Show Run's roster, not from your account generally.

**Home:** wherever the Show is being staged (Catharsis, First Theater, Victory Theater), plus Greenroom for Character management and Storyboards/Library for prep between sessions.

**Common actions:** play your selected Character during live sessions, act on stage objects your Character can perceive/interact with, use in-character chat.

**What it does not control:** other Characters' private information, Scene authoring, cues, or roster management — those belong to Director/Producer. Being Cast on one Show doesn't make you Cast on another; each Show Run's roster is independent.

**One important detail:** whichever Character you have *selected* is what determines your participation — not your account, and not some other "currently active" persona elsewhere in Victory.

**Contextual help:** Guide content appears in Greenroom (Character setup) and when you first join a live session.
